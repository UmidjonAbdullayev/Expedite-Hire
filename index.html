About Us

Established in 2020, Expedite Hire is an independent and trusted partner, proud to provide support every step of the way. For drivers, we focus on finding the right job with reliable companies. For carriers, we make hiring top talent easy and stress-free.

We take the time to understand each driver’s needs and work to match them with employers who fit. Clear and honest communication and no payment concerns are guaranteed.

Whether you’re looking to drive or hire, we are committed to helping you succeed!

For Drivers

Whether you’re an owner-operator, lease operator, company driver, or looking to rent a truck – we will connect you with top companies offering competitive pay and great opportunities. We take the time to understand your experience, goals, and preferences so you get matched with a job that’s right for you.
